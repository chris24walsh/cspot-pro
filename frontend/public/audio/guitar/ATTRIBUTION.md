# Guitar samples

Steel-string acoustic guitar and muted guitar notes from the **FluidR3_GM**
soundfont, distributed as pre-rendered MP3 notes by **gleitz / MIDI.js Soundfonts**.

Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Bank: https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/
License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Selected original MP3 notes have been extracted from the bank's JavaScript
containers without changing their audio. Runtime playback may transpose,
attenuate and fade them. The bank's documented license is in the source README:
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts#soundfonts-available
